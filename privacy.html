Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

ShopHop ("we," "us") is an iOS app for discovering garage sales and buying and selling items locally. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using ShopHop, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password (stored securely by our backend provider), and an optional profile photo.
  • Listings and content you create: item photos, titles, descriptions, prices, and garage sale details you choose to post.
  • Location: with your permission, your device location is used to show nearby sales and listings on the map. Listing locations shown to other users are approximate (displayed as a general area, not your exact address) until you choose to share more.
  • Messages: chats between buyers and sellers are stored so we can deliver them between devices and investigate reports of abuse.
  • Purchases and subscriptions: if you subscribe to ShopHop Plus, the purchase is processed by Apple. We and our subscription partner (RevenueCat) receive transaction records (such as product, price, and status) — we never see your payment card details.
  • Identity verification (optional): if you choose to verify your identity for a verified badge, verification is performed by Stripe. Stripe collects your ID document and a selfie and tells us only the result (verified or not). We do not receive or store copies of your ID documents.
  • Device and usage data: push notification tokens (so we can deliver notifications you've enabled) and basic technical logs used to keep the service running and secure.

How we use information

  • To operate the marketplace: showing listings, powering search and the map, and delivering messages and notifications.
  • To process ShopHop Plus subscriptions and unlock subscriber features.
  • To keep the community safe: investigating reports, preventing fraud and spam, and enforcing our Terms of Service.
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share information with

  • Other users: your profile name, photo, listings, and approximate listing location are visible to other users. Your email and exact location are not.
  • Service providers: we use Supabase (database, authentication, and storage), Stripe (optional identity verification), RevenueCat (subscription management), Apple (payments and push notifications), and Expo (app services). These providers process data only to provide their services to us.
  • Legal: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app, or by emailing SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE from your account email with the subject "Delete my account." When your account is deleted, your profile, listings, and messages are removed from active systems, subject to limited retention required for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Your choices

  • Location: you can disable location access in iOS Settings; the map will have limited functionality.
  • Notifications: you can disable push notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Verification: identity verification is optional.
  • Access and correction: you can edit your profile in the app, or contact us to access or correct your information.

Meeting in person

ShopHop connects local buyers and sellers who complete transactions in person. Use common sense when meeting: public places, daylight hours, and bringing a friend are always good ideas. We do not conduct background checks on users.

Children

ShopHop is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard providers and safeguards (encrypted connections, access controls) to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date, and notify you in the app when appropriate.
